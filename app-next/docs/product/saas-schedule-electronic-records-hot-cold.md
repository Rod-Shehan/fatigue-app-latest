# SaaS schedule draft — Electronic fatigue records (hot / cold access)

## 1. Definitions (plain English)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Electronic Record** | The attested fatigue / work-diary information held in Circadia as structured data (including event timeline content), together with the applicable **signature image** and attestation timestamp, and related amendment / audit history needed to interpret that attestation. |
| **Live Service** | The online Circadia application environment used for day-to-day logging, compliance views, and Enterprise browsing of records that remain on the live database. |
| **Long-Term Storage** | Circadia-controlled secure storage of Electronic Records (and encrypted database copies) retained for the Retention Period, which may not be instantly queryable in the Live Service. |
| **Retrieval Request** | A request by an authorised Customer user for Circadia to retrieve and produce Electronic Records from Long-Term Storage (or to produce a formal pack from the Live Service when requested for audit / legal hold). |
| **SoR Pack** | Circadia’s standard production of the Electronic Record (machine-readable export including structured data, signature, attestation time, and audit). A printable PDF may be supplied as an optional **reproduction** generated from the Electronic Record. |
| **Retention Period** | At least **three (3) years** from the relevant last entry / record date, or such longer period as applicable law or the Order Form requires. |

## 2. Nature of the record

2.1 Circadia maintains the Customer’s fatigue / work-diary information as an **Electronic Record**.

2.2 The Customer acknowledges that:

(a) a **PDF or printed trip sheet** generated by Circadia (or filed by the Customer in SharePoint, email, or similar) is a **convenient view or copy**, and is **not** Circadia’s primary electronic system of record unless the Agreement expressly provides otherwise; and  

(b) Circadia can regenerate printable documents from the Electronic Record when reasonably required.

2.3 Circadia will take reasonable steps so that, during the Retention Period, it can **produce** the Electronic Record to the Customer and, where the Customer lawfully requires and Circadia is permitted to do so, support production to regulators or courts.

---

## 3. Live Service vs Long-Term Storage

3.1 **Working (live) access.** Circadia keeps a working set of records available for immediate use in the Live Service (for example, current and recent work weeks used for logging, compliance checks, and ordinary Enterprise filters).

3.2 **Pilot / current commercial posture (H1).** Until Circadia notifies the Customer of a change (for example when multi-fleet scale requires graduation), Circadia may keep **all signed Electronic Records on the Live Service**. That does not reduce the Retention Period or Circadia’s duty to retain independent copies in Long-Term Storage.

3.3 **Graduation (future).** Circadia may move older Electronic Records from the Live Service into Long-Term Storage for cost and performance reasons. Circadia will not treat Customer-held PDFs as a substitute for Circadia’s Electronic Record when doing so.

3.4 **No infinite instant history.** The Live Service is **not** warranted to provide instant, interactive access to every day of the entire Retention Period through date pickers or similar UI. Records outside the live working set remain **retained and retrievable** under clause 4.

3.5 **Off-site copies.** Circadia maintains encrypted off-site copies of the production database (or equivalent Electronic Record archives) as part of Long-Term Storage, with retention aligned to the Retention Period (not less than three years unless law or the Order Form requires longer).

---

## 4. Retrieval Requests

4.1 **Who may request.** Retrieval Requests may be submitted by the Customer’s **tenant owner** account, by Circadia operations on the Customer’s instruction, or by named managers **if** the owner has delegated that authority in the product or in writing (H3).

4.2 **How.** Requests are made through the Circadia Enterprise / manager facilities provided for older records (or by written instruction to Circadia support if the product path is unavailable).

4.3 **Standard SLA (H2).** Circadia will use reasonable efforts to fulfil a standard Retrieval Request within **two (2) business days**, measured in Australian Western Standard Time (AWST) business days, after receiving a complete request (range, reason, and authorised requester).

4.4 **Urgent requests.** Where the Customer notifies Circadia that the request is for an urgent **legal hold** or **regulator produce**, Circadia will use reasonable efforts to escalate **same day** when practicable. This is a best-efforts escalation, not a guarantee of same-day completion in all circumstances.

4.5 **What is delivered (H4).** Circadia’s standard fulfilment is an **SoR Pack**. Any PDF supplied with the pack is labelled or described as a **reproduction from the Electronic Record**, not as a replacement for it.

4.6 **Fair use — included (H7).** Reasonable Retrieval Requests for ordinary audit, investigation, or compliance produce are **included** in the subscription, subject to any fair-use volume stated in the Order Form.

4.7 **Extraordinary requests — chargeable (H7).** The following may be charged as professional services or at rates in the Order Form (or Circadia’s then-current rates if none stated):

(a) whole-database or whole-platform forensic restores;  

(b) bulk litigation export programmes beyond fair use;  

(c) repeated or abusive retrieval volumes that materially exceed ordinary operational use.

---

## 5. Customer responsibilities

5.1 Unless the Agreement expressly appoints Circadia as the sole record keeper, the **Customer remains the record keeper** under applicable fatigue, WHS, heavy vehicle, and related laws.

5.2 The Customer should use Circadia’s produce and Retrieval Request channels for audits and legal holds, and should **not** assume that a local PDF folder or SharePoint library is a complete Circadia Electronic Record.

5.3 The Customer must notify Circadia **promptly** of any legal hold or preservation duty that requires Circadia to pause deletion, graduation, or other disposition of identified Electronic Records.

5.4 The Customer is responsible for authorising who may submit Retrieval Requests and for keeping owner / manager accounts secure.

---

## 6. What this schedule does not promise

For clarity, Circadia does **not** promise under this schedule:

(a) that every date in the Retention Period will be selectable and instantly viewable in Enterprise as if it were the current work week;  

(b) that PDF publication to SharePoint, email, or similar is Circadia’s authoritative archive; or  

(c) unlimited free forensic restoration of the entire platform outside fair-use Retrieval Requests (see clause 4.7).

## 8. Customer-facing explanation (optional schedule annex / help text)

*May be reused in UI help or onboarding (aligned with product copy):*

> Recent records are available immediately in Circadia.  
> Older retained records are kept safely in long-term storage so legal retention can be met without keeping every year on the live system forever.  
> If you need those older records, request them from Enterprise (Older records) or Support. We retrieve and reassemble them — usually within about two business days (Perth).  
> The authoritative record is your signed electronic data (what was logged and the signature), not a PDF file. We can produce a PDF from that data when you need a printable copy.
